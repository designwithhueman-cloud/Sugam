Why we bake at 2am

Our dough rests for 36 hours. That long, cold proof is why the crust shatters and the crumb stays open. It also means the bread has to leave the oven at 5:30, so our riders leave at 6.

Ruth, who runs the night shift, has been shaping loaves this way since 2011. She will tell you the rye starter is older than her flat.

How a delivery works

  1. Pick a loaf and a day by 9pm. Most people choose the country white.
  2. We text you when the rider leaves the bakery, usually around 6:10.
  3. The loaf arrives in a paper bag on your step. No subscription, no minimum order.

Outside our 11 postcodes? Collect from the bakery from 7am.
